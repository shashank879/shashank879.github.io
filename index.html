I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the University of Bath, working with Dr. Vinay Namboodiri and Dr. Janina Hoffmann. My research focuses on developing automated goal-oriented reinforcement learning agents that can learn skills, plan hierarchically, and discover goals autonomously.

Prior to my Ph.D., I was a Pre-Doc Researcher at Google, where I developed semi-supervised learning methods for satellite image segmentation. I have also worked extensively in natural language processing, achieving top rankings on the Stanford SQuAD leaderboards.

Research Interests

My research lies at the intersection of machine learning and artificial intelligence, with a particular focus on:
• Hierarchical Reinforcement Learning
• Goal-Oriented AI Systems
• Skill Discovery and Planning
• Computer Vision and Remote Sensing
• Semi-Supervised Learning
