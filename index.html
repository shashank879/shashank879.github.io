I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the University of Bath, working with Dr. Vinay Namboodiri and Dr. Janina Hoffmann. My research focuses on developing automated goal-oriented reinforcement learning agents that can learn skills, plan hierarchically, and discover goals autonomously.

Prior to my Ph.D., I was a Pre-Doc Researcher at Google, where I developed semi-supervised learning methods for satellite image segmentation. I have also worked extensively in natural language processing, achieving top rankings on the Stanford SQuAD leaderboards.

Selected Publications

Options-based Large Language Models (OLLM)
Under Review
Shashank Sharma, et al.
Proposed a lightweight architecture that replaces single next-token prediction with a set of learned options indexed by a discrete latent variable, enabling explicit modeling of variation without temperature sampling. Applied to a 1.7B-parameter backbone with only 1.56% trainable parameters, achieving ~70% correctness on OmniMath under optimal latent selection (vs. 51% LoRA baseline), while a learned policy in the latent space provides efficient reward optimization and reduces misalignments without additional KL penalties.

Discrete Hierarchical Planning (DHP) for HRL Agents
NeurIPS'25 ARLET & Efficient Reasoning Workshops
Shashank Sharma, Vinay Namboodiri, Janina Hoffmann
Replaced continuous distance metrics with discrete reachability checks for subgoal planning, recursively constructing tree-structured plans that decompose long-term goals into simpler subtasks. Achieved 100% success rate in 25-room visual navigation environments (vs. 90% baseline), using a novel advantage estimation strategy that inherenetly encourages shorter plans, balanced plan trees, and generalizes beyond the maximum tree depth. The method also introduces a novel memory-based exploration technique that outperforms expert data.
Paper
Multi-Resolution Skill Discovery for Hierarchical Reinforcement Learning
NeurIPS'23 Goal-Conditioned Reinforcement Learning Workshop
Shashank Sharma, Vinay Namboodiri, Janina Hoffmann
Introduced a end-to-end training hierarchical RL framework where multiple skill modules predict subgoals at different temporal horizons (8, 16, 32, 64 steps), along with a learned meta-controller selecting the appropriate resolution based on task context. Achieved consistent improvements over single-resolution baselines on DeepMind Control Suite, Gym-Robotics, and long-horizon AntMaze tasks, demonstrating that temporal partitioning of skills provides a useful inductive bias for HRL.
Paper
Evaluating Self and Semi-Supervised Methods for Remote Sensing Segmentation Tasks
Remote Sensing Applications, 2021
Chaitanya Patel*, Shashank Sharma*, Valerie J. Pasquarella, Varun Gulshan
Comprehensive evaluation of self and semi supervised learning methods for satellite image segmentation in water management applications. Demonstrated that the approaches achieve baseline performance with only 1% labeled data and improve AUC-PR by 5% when utilizing unlabeled data.
Paper
No Modes Left Behind: Capturing the Data Distribution Effectively Using GANs
AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence, 2018 (Oral)
Shashank Sharma, Vinay P. Namboodiri
Directly addresses mode collapse in GANs with a novel pair-wise gradient penalty method that ensures comprehensive data distribution coverage. Validated on toy datasets, surveillance video data (highly susceptible to mode collapse), and CelebA, demonstrating superior performance in capturing minor modes that traditional GANs frequently miss.
